Frequently asked

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our standard tiers start at 25 units. Smaller one-off orders can usually be accommodated. Ask us directly.

Do you offer rush turnaround?

Yes, for a subset of our catalogue. Tell us your deadline in the enquiry form and we will confirm feasibility within 24 hours.

Is GST included in the pricing?

Unit prices are shown before GST. Every order ships with a compliant GST invoice.

Can we ship to multiple addresses in one order?

Yes. This is common for distributed and remote teams. Share your address list and we will handle the split.

Which branding methods do you offer?

Laser engraving, UV and full-colour print, foil stamping and embroidery, matched to each product.

GST and compliance

Plan gifting budgets GST-inclusive. Input Tax Credit on gifts is generally blocked under Section 17(5)(h) of the CGST Act.

Employee gifts above Rs 50,000 in a financial year, and business gifts to partners above Rs 20,000, have specific tax treatment. Please confirm with your own tax advisor.
