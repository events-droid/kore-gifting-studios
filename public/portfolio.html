How a project runs

From brief to delivery, in five steps.

  1. Brief

    Tell us the occasion, the number of recipients, the budget and the delivery dates.

  2. Build

    Choose the box, filling, products, colours and personalisation. Our 3D preview shows the result as you go.

  3. Quote

    We confirm stock and price, then send a quotation with GST and delivery, plus a digital mockup.

  4. Approve

    You approve the mockup and quotation. Nothing is produced before that.

  5. Deliver

    We pack, label and dispatch to one address or many, and share tracking with your team.
